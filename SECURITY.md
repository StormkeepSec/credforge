# Security policy

credforge signs credentials on behalf of the organizations that use it, so we treat security
reports as a priority.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue. Email **security@credforge.app** with:

- what you found and where (URL, endpoint or file),
- steps to reproduce, and
- the impact you believe it has.

We'll acknowledge your report within 3 business days and keep you updated until it's resolved.
Please give us a reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

## Scope

In scope: this repository and the hosted instance at https://credforge.app. Especially valued:
credential forgery or tampering, signing-key exposure, cross-organization data access,
authentication bypass, and leaks of recipient email addresses.

Out of scope: denial-of-service testing against credforge.app, social engineering, and
findings that need a compromised device.
