## Website section copy

### Give achievement a clear shape
Turn a skill, milestone, or accomplishment into a badge people can recognize. Start with what was earned and make its meaning clear.

### Award with purpose
Recognition lands differently when it reflects real work. Use credforge to create and award digital badges for moments that matter to your program and its people.

### Build on an open standard
Open Badges 3.0 provides a standards-based way to describe an achievement, its issuer, recipient, criteria, and possible evidence. credforge is built for creating and awarding badges in that format.

## Language guardrails

- Say **create and award**; these are the confirmed platform actions.
- Lead with the achievement, then explain the badge.
- Use **Open Badges 3.0** with this capitalization and spacing.
- Do not claim 1EdTech certification, universal acceptance, analytics, integrations, or automatic verification without product evidence.
- The standard supports claims about structured achievement information and verifiable credentials; validate the implementation before making platform-specific technical claims.

**Standards source:** https://standards.1edtech.org/open-badges/specifications/standards/v3p0
